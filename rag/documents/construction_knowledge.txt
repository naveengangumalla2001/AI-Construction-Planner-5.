Residential Construction Knowledge

Construction generally consists of several major stages.

1. Site Preparation
Site preparation includes clearing the site, leveling the ground,
and preparing the area for foundation work.

2. Foundation
The foundation transfers the building load to the ground.
Foundation design depends on soil conditions, building loads,
site conditions, and engineering requirements.

3. Structural Work
Structural work includes columns, beams, slabs, and other
load-bearing components.

4. Brick and Plaster Work
Walls are constructed using appropriate masonry materials.
Plastering provides a finished surface for walls and ceilings.

5. Electrical and Plumbing
Electrical systems provide power distribution throughout the building.
Plumbing systems provide water supply and drainage.

6. Flooring and Painting
Flooring materials may include tiles, stone, wood, or other materials.
Painting provides protection and finishing to walls and surfaces.

7. Final Finishing
Final finishing can include doors, windows, fixtures, fittings,
cleaning, and other finishing activities.

Construction Cost Factors

Construction cost can depend on built-up area, location,
number of floors, construction quality, material quality,
labour cost, material prices, design complexity, and site conditions.

Construction Safety

Construction activities should follow applicable safety standards.
Structural, electrical, fire-safety, and other technical decisions
should be reviewed by qualified professionals.
